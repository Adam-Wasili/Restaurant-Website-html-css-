Our story

Why "Dziwe"?

Dziwe means "pool" or "lake" in Chichewa — and that's exactly where this kitchen started. Long before there was a restaurant, there were Sunday trips to the lakeshore, where chambo was bought straight off the fishermen's boats and cooked the same afternoon over charcoal.

We brought that same habit to Mangochi: simple ingredients, cooked properly, served without ceremony. Every dish on our menu traces back to a recipe someone's grandmother actually used — we've just written them down and added a few new ones along the way.

Today Dziwe is open seven days a week, serving breakfast, lunch, and dinner to neighbours, visitors, and anyone craving something that tastes like a trip to the lake.

Milestones

  • 2019

    The first pot of ndiwo

    Dziwe began as a small weekend stall, cooking three dishes a day for the neighbourhood.

  • 2022

    Moved to Glyn Jones Road

    Demand outgrew the stall, and Dziwe found a permanent home with a proper kitchen and dining room.

  • 2026

    Online ordering arrives

    You can now send your order straight to our kitchen from the menu page — no phone call required.
